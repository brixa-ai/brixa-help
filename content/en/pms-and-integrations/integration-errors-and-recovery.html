PMS Issues and Recovery

When your PMS is temporarily unreachable, Brixa retries on its own and brings staff in rather than guessing — here is how to recognize a PMS issue and what to do about it.

How a PMS issue shows up

  • Quotes can't confirm availability. A guest asks about a stay, but Brixa cannot check your PMS to build room options, so the quote stalls instead of going out with unverified rooms.
  • A reservation didn't appear in the PMS. A guest accepted a quote, but the automatic reservation was not created because the PMS could not be reached at that moment.
  • Availability looks out of date. Note that a few minutes of lag after a recent PMS change is normal and not an error — see What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

What Brixa does automatically

Brixa never invents availability or prices. When the PMS does not respond, it:

  • Retries the connection. Most outages are brief, and the next attempt often succeeds without anyone noticing.
  • Asks staff to step in when a guest is waiting. If retrying would leave a guest hanging, Brixa flags the conversation for your team instead of guessing. These flags appear alongside other cases needing human attention — see When Brixa Escalates.

What staff should do

  1. Check your PMS directly. Confirm it is up and that the rooms or reservation in question look the way you expect. Your PMS is always the source of truth.
  2. Answer the waiting guest. If Brixa flagged a conversation, reply to the guest yourself using what you see in the PMS — availability, prices, or a booking confirmation.
  3. Enter urgent reservations manually. If a guest accepted a quote and the reservation has not appeared in your PMS, create it there by hand using the details from the booking in Brixa. A manual entry now beats a delayed one later.
  4. Contact Brixa support if it persists. Include the conversation reference (the guest conversation where the problem appeared) so the team can trace exactly what happened.

When things recover on their own

Brief outages usually resolve without any action: once the PMS is reachable again, new quotes check availability normally and new acceptances create reservations as usual. Work that was flagged to staff during the outage stays with staff — Brixa does not retroactively send quotes or create reservations you have already handled manually, so there is no risk of duplicates from simply waiting. After any incident, a quick comparison of recent bookings between Brixa and your PMS is good practice; if something still looks off, see Reservation and Sync Issues.
