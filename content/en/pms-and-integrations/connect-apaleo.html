Connect Apaleo

If your hotel runs on Apaleo, you can connect it to Brixa yourself from Hotel Info. Brixa then quotes from your live Apaleo availability and prices and places accepted bookings in Apaleo.

What you need

  • A Brixa login that can edit Hotel Info.
  • An Apaleo user who can sign in to your Apaleo account. That user needs rights to read the property setup, rates and reservations, and to manage reservations.

Connect your property

  1. In Brixa, open Hotel Info and go to the PMS section. When no PMS is connected, you see two tiles: Mews and Apaleo.
  2. Choose Apaleo. A short description appears with a Connect with Apaleo button.
  3. Select Connect with Apaleo. Your browser goes to Apaleo.
  4. Sign in with your Apaleo account and approve the access Brixa asks for.
  5. Back in Brixa, if your Apaleo account has several properties, pick the property that belongs to this hotel from the list, then select Confirm property.

The section now shows the connection as connected. The sync controls for room types, rate plans, cancellation policies and age categories work the same way as with any other PMS. See What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

What Brixa reads and writes in Apaleo

Brixa readsBrixa writes
Your properties, room types (called unit groups in Apaleo), rate plans with their cancellation and no-show terms, availability and prices for quotes, and reservations (so a returning guest can be recognised by booking number and surname).The booking, when a guest accepts and pays or leaves a card, and a cancellation when your staff cancel from Brixa.

How bookings work with Apaleo

Apaleo does not hold rooms tentatively. When a guest accepts a quote, it shows Awaiting Guarantee, and the booking is created in Apaleo only once the guest's payment or card has come through. To collect that payment, connect a payment option; see Collect Payments with apaleo Pay and Ask Guests to Pay or Leave a Card at Booking.

Photos

Apaleo does not provide room photos. Upload room type and hotel photos in Brixa, as you do today.

Disconnect or reconnect

In the same PMS section, select Disconnect to remove the connection. To connect again, repeat the steps above, including the Apaleo sign-in and approval.
