Connect Apaleo

If your hotel runs on Apaleo, you can connect it to Brixa yourself from the hotel's settings page. Brixa then quotes from your live Apaleo availability and prices and places accepted bookings in Apaleo.

What you need

  • Access to the hotel's settings page in Brixa.
  • An Apaleo user who can sign in to your Apaleo account. That user needs rights to read the property setup, rates and reservations, and to manage reservations.

Connect your property

  1. In Brixa, select Hotels in the left menu, open the hotel's settings page and go to the PMS Integration section. When no PMS is connected, it asks Which system does this hotel use? and shows two tiles: Mews and Apaleo.
  2. Choose Apaleo. The tile reads Sign in to Apaleo and approve the connection. and offers a Connect with Apaleo button.
  3. Select Connect with Apaleo. Your browser goes to Apaleo.
  4. Sign in with your Apaleo account and approve the access Brixa asks for.
  5. Back in Brixa, if your Apaleo account has several properties, pick the property under Choose the property for this hotel, then select Connect to selected property.

The section now shows the connection as connected. The sync controls in the same section work as before. See What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

What Brixa reads and writes in Apaleo

Brixa reads:

  • Your properties and room types.
  • Rate plans with their cancellation and no-show terms.
  • Availability and prices for quotes.
  • Reservations, so a returning guest can be recognised by booking number and surname.

Brixa writes:

  • The booking, when a guest accepts and pays or leaves a card.
  • A cancellation, when your staff cancel from Brixa.

How bookings work with Apaleo

Apaleo does not hold rooms tentatively. When a guest accepts a quote, it shows Awaiting Guarantee, and the booking is created in Apaleo only once the guest's payment or card has come through. To collect that payment, connect a payment option; see Collect Payments with apaleo Pay and Ask Guests to Pay or Leave a Card at Booking.

Photos

Apaleo does not provide room photos. Upload room type and hotel photos in Brixa, as you do today.

Disconnect or reconnect

In the same PMS Integration section, select Disconnect to remove the connection. To connect again, repeat the steps above, including the Apaleo sign-in and approval.
