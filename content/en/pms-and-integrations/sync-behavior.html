What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS

Quick answers to the questions hotels ask most about the PMS connection: what Brixa will and will not touch, where bookings appear, and which system to trust.

Will Brixa change my rates or setup?

No. Brixa never:

  • Modifies your rates, rate plans, or pricing rules
  • Adds, removes, or reconfigures room types
  • Edits or cancels reservations that already exist in your PMS
  • Blocks inventory or changes restrictions

The only thing Brixa ever creates in your PMS is a new reservation from an accepted quote — and only if you have enabled that.

Do quotes reflect my real availability?

Yes. Quote options are based on what your PMS shows as available for the requested dates, and they refresh when the guest changes dates or party size. If a quote offers a room your PMS says is sold out, or misses one that is open, check your PMS first and then tell Brixa's team.

Where do new bookings appear?

In your PMS, when automatic reservation creation is enabled for your hotel. The reservation carries what the guest agreed to — dates, the selected room, guest count, and price — along with the guest's contact details, and its notes include any special requests from the conversation, so your front desk works from the PMS as usual. If automatic creation is not enabled, your team enters accepted bookings manually from the booking view in Brixa. See From Accepted Quote to Reservation for the full picture.

Do I need to trigger anything?

No. There are no exports to run, buttons to press, or sync schedules to manage. Your day-to-day PMS work does not change.

Which system should I trust?

Your PMS is always the source of truth. It is the record that counts for operations, billing, and reporting. If Brixa and your PMS ever seem to disagree, trust the PMS and see PMS Issues and Recovery for what to do next.
