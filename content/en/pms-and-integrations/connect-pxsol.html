Connect Pxsol

If your hotel runs on Pxsol, you can connect it to Brixa yourself from the hotel's settings page, with the hotel's Pxsol ID and phone number. Once connected, Brixa brings in your room types, rate plans and cancellation policies, and places accepted bookings in Pxsol as pending reservations for you to confirm.

What you need

  • Brixa activated for the hotel in the Pxsol Marketplace. If you do not see it there, ask Pxsol support.
  • The hotel's Pxsol ID, the number Pxsol shows for the property. You can find it in the property's settings in Pxsol; if you cannot, ask Pxsol support.
  • The hotel's phone number, exactly as it appears in the property's contact details in Pxsol.
  • Access to the hotel's settings page in Brixa.

Connect from Brixa

  1. In Brixa, select Hotels in the left menu, open the hotel's settings page and go to the PMS Integration section.
  2. Choose Pxsol.
  3. Enter the hotel's Pxsol ID and phone number, then select Connect.

If Brixa tells you the hotel is not enabled for Brixa, check that Brixa is activated in the Pxsol Marketplace. If it says the phone does not match, compare it with the phone in the property's contact details in Pxsol and enter it exactly as it appears there.

What syncs

  • Your room types, with their photos, how many adults, children and babies each one takes, and the bed options.
  • Your rate plans and their cancellation policies.
  • Your guest age ranges (babies, children and adults), as set in Pxsol.

The sync controls in the PMS Integration section work as with any connected PMS. See What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

How bookings are confirmed

With Manage reservations switched on, when a guest accepts a quote Brixa places a pending reservation in Pxsol and sends the guest Pxsol's secure payment page.

  • Even after the guest completes the payment page, the reservation stays pending until you confirm it in Pxsol. Until then, Brixa shows the quote as Awaiting Guarantee.
  • If the guest does not complete the payment page before the quote expires, Brixa releases the pending reservation and the room goes back on sale.

Manage reservations

Brixa can only place reservations in Pxsol when the Manage reservations switch in the PMS Integration section is on. Switch it on when you are ready for accepted bookings to appear in Pxsol.

Disconnect

Go to the hotel's settings page, open the PMS Integration section and select Disconnect. To connect again, repeat the steps above.
