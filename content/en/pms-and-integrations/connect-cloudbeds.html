Connect Cloudbeds

If your hotel runs on Cloudbeds, you can connect it to Brixa yourself, either from the hotel's settings page in Brixa or from the Cloudbeds Marketplace. Once connected, Brixa brings in your room types and rate plans and places accepted bookings in Cloudbeds.

What you need

  • Access to the hotel's settings page in Brixa.
  • A Cloudbeds user whose role includes Rates and Policies. Without it, some details stay hidden from Brixa and the setup is incomplete.

Connect from Brixa

  1. In Brixa, select Hotels in the left menu, open the hotel's settings page and go to the PMS Integration section.
  2. Choose Cloudbeds, then select Connect with Cloudbeds. Your browser goes to Cloudbeds.
  3. Sign in to Cloudbeds and approve the connection.
  4. Back in Brixa, if your Cloudbeds account has several properties, pick the one for this hotel.

Connect from the Cloudbeds Marketplace

In the Cloudbeds Marketplace, select Connect on the Brixa listing. This opens Brixa: sign in to Brixa and pick the hotel you want to connect.

What syncs

  • Your room types, with their photos and descriptions, and how many adults and children each one takes.
  • Your rate plans.
  • Your cancellation policies. See the next section.

Rates become bookable through Brixa once their cancellation policies have been read from Cloudbeds.

The sync controls in the PMS Integration section work as before. See What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

Cancellation policies and test bookings

Cloudbeds does not let Brixa look up your cancellation policies directly, so Brixa reads them through test reservations that it cancels immediately. In Cloudbeds they appear as cancelled reservations named Brixa Policy-Check. No fee is charged and no email is sent.

  • Test reservations need Manage reservations to be switched on in the PMS Integration section. Switch it on, then sync again. Until then Brixa cannot read your policies, and the rates stay unavailable.
  • If you change a policy in Cloudbeds, sync again so Brixa picks up the change. Until you do, Brixa keeps using the previous terms.
  • If none of the rates using a policy can be booked in Cloudbeds, the policy stays inactive and those rates stay unavailable in Brixa. Make sure one of them can be booked, then sync again.

Notes that need attention

After a sync, Brixa may show a note under Needs attention. The most common ones:

  • Cloudbeds cancellation policies are read with a test booking. Turn on Manage reservations… Switch on Manage reservations in the PMS Integration section, then sync Cancellation policies again.
  • The following cancellation policy will remain inactive… Brixa could not read that policy. Check that a rate using it can be booked in Cloudbeds, then sync again.
  • A Brixa test booking could not be cancelled automatically… Find the reservation named Brixa Policy-Check in Cloudbeds and cancel it yourself.

The age sync shows a blue note instead: Cloudbeds doesn't share age ranges with partners yet… It is information, not a problem, and appears on every age sync. Set up the age groups as described in the next section.

Children and ages

Cloudbeds knows two kinds of guests, adults and children, and does not share age ranges with Brixa. Brixa quotes and books both, including the extra charges you set in Cloudbeds for additional adults and children. Set up the age groups in Brixa yourself:

  1. Open the hotel's settings in Brixa and go to the guest age groups.
  2. Disable Infants. Cloudbeds treats a baby as a child.
  3. Set the child age limit to match your hotel's child policy. Adults start from the next age.
  4. If your hotel does not take children, disable Children as well.

How bookings work with Cloudbeds

Brixa holds rooms in Cloudbeds the way it does in other PMSs that support holds. With Manage reservations switched on, what happens when a guest accepts a quote depends on the quote's cancellation policy (see Ask Guests to Pay or Leave a Card at Booking):

  • When the policy asks the guest to pay or leave a card, or is set to Per the payment instructions, the quote shows Awaiting Guarantee and the reservation is created in Cloudbeds in Confirmation Pending status, which holds the room. Brixa confirms it once the payment or card has come through; to collect it through Brixa, connect Stripe (see Connect Stripe). If you collect the guarantee yourself, confirm the quote in Brixa after you have collected it and Brixa confirms the reservation in Cloudbeds.
  • When the policy asks for nothing, the reservation is created as confirmed as soon as the guest accepts, with no Awaiting Guarantee stage.
  • If the quote expires without a guarantee, Brixa cancels the pending reservation in Cloudbeds and the room goes back on sale.

Brixa's pending reservations appear in your Confirmation Pending list in Cloudbeds. If you confirm one there, the quote becomes confirmed in Brixa and the guest's open payment link stops working, so do this only after you have collected the guarantee yourself. In the Cloudbeds activity log, a reservation Brixa holds can show as confirmed for a moment before it turns pending. This is expected.

In every case:

  • Brixa books on the Website/Booking Engine source, with Cash as the payment method. Payments collected through Stripe stay in Stripe; see Payments at Check-in.
  • Cloudbeds does not send its own confirmation email for these bookings. Brixa sends the guest messages.
  • If your property charges a fee that is fixed per reservation, Brixa does not create bookings with more than one room in Cloudbeds. Your team gets an alert and enters the booking in Cloudbeds. Bookings with one room are not affected.

Cancellations

When your team cancels a reservation in Cloudbeds, the cancellation shows up in Brixa as well.

Disconnect

Go to the hotel's settings page, open the PMS Integration section and select Disconnect. To connect again, repeat the steps above.
