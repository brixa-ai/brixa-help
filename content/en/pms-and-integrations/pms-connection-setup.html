Connecting Your PMS

Connecting your property management system lets Brixa quote from your live availability and, when you choose to enable it, place accepted bookings in your PMS automatically. Here is what you need and the steps to get set up.

If your PMS is Apaleo or Cloudbeds, you can connect it yourself: see Connect Apaleo or Connect Cloudbeds.

What the connection gives you

  • Quotes based on your real availability. Guests are offered rooms that are actually open for their dates, not options from a static room list.
  • Accepted bookings in your PMS, automatically. No manual re-typing of reservations. This is optional and switched on separately — see below.

What you need before setup

For PMSs other than Apaleo and Cloudbeds, the connection uses credentials issued by your PMS provider. How you obtain them depends on your PMS; Mews is one example of a supported system. Your PMS provider's support team or account manager can issue or approve the access, and Brixa's team will tell you exactly what to request.

How setup works

For other PMSs, such as Mews, setup is done together with Brixa's team rather than through a self-service screen:

  1. Contact Brixa and let them know which PMS your hotel uses.
  2. Obtain access from your PMS provider. Brixa's team will guide you through requesting the right credentials or authorization.
  3. Brixa's team completes the connection for your hotel and confirms it with you.

Verifying the connection works

The simplest check is a real quote. With a team-assisted setup, ask Brixa's team to confirm the connection; with any PMS, watch the first few guest requests: quotes should offer room options that match what your PMS shows as available for those dates. If a quote offers a room your PMS says is sold out (or misses one that is open), tell Brixa's team (or check your connection settings if you connected Apaleo or Cloudbeds yourself).

Enabling automatic bookings when you're ready

Many hotels start with quoting only: guests get quotes from live availability, and staff enter accepted bookings into the PMS themselves. Cloudbeds is the exception: switch on Manage reservations from the start, because Brixa needs it to read your cancellation policies and rates stay unavailable until then (see Connect Cloudbeds). With any other PMS, once you have seen accurate quotes and trust the flow, switch on Manage reservations in the hotel's PMS Integration section — from then on, accepted bookings appear in your PMS without manual entry (for Apaleo and Cloudbeds, see Connect Apaleo and Connect Cloudbeds for when the booking is created). If anything ever goes wrong with the connection, see PMS Issues and Recovery.
