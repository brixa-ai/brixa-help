Connecting Your PMS

Connecting your property management system lets Brixa quote from live availability and, when you choose to enable it, create accepted bookings in your PMS automatically.

What the connection enables

Once your PMS is connected, Brixa can do two things it cannot do otherwise:

  • Quote from live availability. When a guest asks about a stay, Brixa checks your PMS for what is actually available for those dates and builds quote options from real inventory — not from a static room list.
  • Create reservations automatically. When a guest accepts a quote and all required details are complete, Brixa can create the reservation directly in your PMS, including the guest's details and any notes from the conversation. This is optional and switched on separately — see below.

What you need before setup

The connection uses credentials issued by your PMS provider — an authorization that allows Brixa to read availability and, if enabled, create reservations. How you obtain these depends on your PMS; Mews is one example of a supported system. Your PMS provider's support team or account manager can issue or approve the access, and Brixa's team will tell you exactly what to request.

How setup works

Setup is done together with Brixa's team rather than through a self-service screen:

  1. Contact Brixa and let them know which PMS your hotel uses.
  2. Obtain access from your PMS provider. Brixa's team will guide you through requesting the right credentials or authorization.
  3. Brixa configures the connection for your hotel and confirms it can read your availability.

Verifying the connection works

The simplest check is a real quote. Ask Brixa's team to confirm the connection during setup, then watch the first few guest requests: quotes should offer room options that match what your PMS shows as available for those dates. If a quote offers a room your PMS says is sold out (or misses one that is open), tell Brixa's team — keeping in mind that very recent PMS changes can take a few minutes to appear, as explained in What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

Enabling reservation writing when you're ready

Reading availability and writing reservations are separate permissions. Many hotels start with availability only: Brixa quotes from live inventory, and staff enter accepted bookings into the PMS themselves. Once you have seen accurate quotes and trust the flow, ask Brixa's team to enable reservation writing — from then on, accepted bookings are created in your PMS automatically. If anything ever goes wrong with the connection, see PMS Issues and Recovery.
