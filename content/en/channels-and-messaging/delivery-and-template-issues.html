Delivery Failures and Template Issues

Sometimes a message to a guest cannot be delivered — the window closed, a template was rejected, the guest is unreachable, or the channel itself is having problems. This article explains what a failure means, what Brixa does about it, and what your team should do next.

Why a message can fail to send

  • The messaging window closed. The channel no longer accepts a free-text reply. On WhatsApp a template message may still work; on Instagram you generally have to wait for the guest to write again. See Messaging Windows and Sending Limits.
  • The guest is unreachable. The guest changed their number, deleted their account, or blocked the hotel. No message will get through on that channel.
  • A template was rejected. Template messages must match their approved wording exactly. If a template is out of date, disabled, or no longer approved, sends that rely on it fail.
  • A channel outage. WhatsApp or Instagram occasionally have service problems on their side. These are usually temporary and affect many hotels at once.

What Brixa does automatically

A single failed attempt rarely needs your attention. When a failure looks temporary — such as an outage — Brixa retries sending on its own. Many messages that fail on the first attempt are delivered shortly afterwards without anyone stepping in.

When delivery keeps failing and Brixa has exhausted its retries, the message is marked as permanently failed and the conversation is flagged for staff attention, so a guest is never silently left without an answer. This works like other cases where Brixa hands a conversation to a human — see When Brixa Escalates.

What staff should do

  1. Open the flagged conversation and check the last message that failed, so you know what the guest was supposed to receive.
  2. Try the conversation again. If the failure was temporary, a fresh reply may go through. If the guest has since written, the window is open again and a normal reply will work.
  3. Use another contact method. If the guest is unreachable on the channel, fall back to whatever else you have — an email address or phone number from the quote or booking details — so an accepted quote or pending question is not lost.
  4. Check the template if failures mention one. Confirm with whoever manages your WhatsApp templates that it is still approved and current.
  5. Contact support if failures continue across several conversations with no obvious cause — that pattern points to a channel or account problem rather than one guest.
