Ask Guests to Pay or Leave a Card at Booking

Each cancellation policy has a setting called What the guest must do to book. Once your Stripe account is connected, it decides whether a guest booking under that policy receives a secure link to pay in full or to leave a card as a guarantee while their room is held.

The three choices

SettingWhat the guest doesTypical use
Nothing — the hotel arranges payment itselfNo link. The message that announces the hold repeats your payment instructions as text, as before. This is the default for every policy.Policies you settle by bank transfer or at the desk.
Pay in full at booking (non-refundable)The guest pays the full quote amount by card. The booking is confirmed once the payment goes through.Non-refundable rates.
Give a card as a guarantee (free cancellation)The guest saves a card. Nothing is charged now; the card stays in your Stripe account for no-shows and late cancellations.Free-cancellation rates.

Deposits or partial amounts are not available: a policy either asks for the full amount, a card, or nothing.

Where to set it

  1. Open Hotel Settings and go to your cancellation policies — the same place where you edit their names and descriptions (see Guest Categories and Policies).
  2. Edit the policy and choose an option under What the guest must do to book.
  3. Save. The choice applies to quotes that reach the hold stage from now on; links already sent keep the terms they were sent with.

This choice is yours alone: a PMS connection updates a policy's description and payment instructions, but never this setting. The setting only takes effect while Stripe is connected under Payments Settings and the card there reads Can accept payments; otherwise Brixa falls back to your payment instructions as text.

What the guest experiences

  1. When the quote reaches Tentatively Booked — the room is on hold — the message announcing the hold carries the link. In your conversation view it appears as Secure your booking; the quote page shows the same action as a button to pay or to add a card.
  2. The link opens a secure checkout page in the guest's language, showing your hotel's name and the stay dates. It works until the end of the day the quote is valid, the same deadline as the hold. A guest who leaves the page can come back and try again before then.
  3. When the payment or the card goes through, Brixa sends the guest a short receipt in the conversation, in their language, and the quote moves to Confirmed by Hotel. If the hotel has a connected PMS, the reservation is confirmed there too. Hotels without a PMS confirm the hold in their own systems as they do today.

Brixa never tells a guest that a payment was received unless it really went through. If a guest says they have paid and you cannot find the payment in Brixa or in Stripe, ask them for their receipt or the last digits of the card they used before you confirm anything.

What you see

On the conversation and on the quote page, a small pill next to the quote status shows where things stand: Awaiting payment or Awaiting card while the link is open, then Paid or Card on file. Hovering over it shows the amount and when it was received; a click opens the payment in Stripe.

If a payment needs a decision from you, the conversation shows an alert like any other escalation (see When Brixa Escalates). The banner tells you what happened and what to do — for example, to send the guest your payment details yourself, to confirm the reservation in your PMS, or to check a payment in Stripe before you confirm the booking or refund the guest. While a payment is waiting for that check, the pill reads Needs review.

Before you rely on it at the desk

Payments and saved cards live in your Stripe account, not in your PMS. Read Check Stripe Before Charging at Check-in so nobody charges a guest twice.
