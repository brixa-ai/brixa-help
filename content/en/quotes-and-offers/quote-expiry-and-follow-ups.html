Quote Expiry and Follow-Ups

Quotes are valid for a limited time, and Brixa nudges quiet guests before that time runs out. This article covers how follow-ups work, what happens when a quote expires, and when to start fresh instead.

Quotes have a validity period

Prices and availability change, so every quote comes with a validity period. While the quote is valid, the guest can open the link, pick an option, and accept. When the period ends without a decision, the quote's status changes to Expired automatically. See Quote Statuses Explained for the full status list.

Automatic follow-ups before expiry

Brixa keeps a follow-up list of quotes that need a nudge: quotes that were sent but got no answer, and quotes stuck waiting on missing guest details. For each one, Brixa prepares a friendly reminder in the guest's own language, tuned to the situation — a first gentle nudge reads differently from a final reminder, and the tone becomes more time-sensitive when check-in is close. Up to three follow-up attempts are made per quote; after the third, Brixa stops chasing and leaves the next move to you. The moment a guest replies, the quote leaves the follow-up list.

What happens when a quote expires

An expired quote is no longer bookable at the quoted prices, but the conversation stays open — the guest can still write to you, and nothing is lost. If the guest comes back and the request hasn't changed, you can reopen the quote by editing it: Brixa rechecks the details and puts it back into the right stage. If dates, occupancy, or prices have moved on, it's better to send a fresh quote.

Seeing and steering follow-up activity

The follow-up list in Brixa shows every quote awaiting a nudge, with the guest's name, channel, stay dates, quote value, how long the guest has been silent, how close check-in is, and which attempt is next (for example "Attempt 2 of 3"). Items are prioritized for you — quotes with check-in only days away rank as urgent. For each item you can:

  1. Open the quote to see the full conversation and details.
  2. Review the suggested follow-up message, shown in your language and the guest's, and edit it before it goes out.
  3. Send the follow-up, which counts one attempt.
  4. Skip the quote to remove it from the list without messaging the guest.

When to send a fresh quote instead

Reopening works best when nothing has changed. Send a new quote when:

  • The guest wants different dates, room types, or a different party size — Brixa reprices the request anyway.
  • Your rates or availability have changed since the original quote, so the old numbers would mislead the guest.
  • The quote used all three follow-up attempts and the guest resurfaces much later — a clean offer restarts the conversation better than a stale one.

For how Brixa builds and sends that new quote, see Creating and Sending Quotes.
