Creating and Sending Quotes

Brixa builds booking quotes directly from what guests write in the chat, so most quotes start themselves. This article explains how a quote takes shape, what it contains, and how to review and send it.

How a quote gets started

When a guest asks about a stay, Brixa reads the message and pulls out the essentials: check-in and check-out dates, the number of adults, children, and infants, and any room preferences. It creates a quote for the conversation and fills in those details automatically. If something important is missing, such as the dates or the number of guests, Brixa asks the guest for it before pricing anything, and the quote shows the status Missing Information until the answer arrives.

You can also start or adjust a quote yourself by editing the booking details on the conversation, for example when a guest called by phone or gave details in a way Brixa could not read.

Quotes with multiple room options

When more than one room arrangement fits the request, Brixa prepares two or three options, each with its own room type, price, and cancellation policy. If your hotel has a connected PMS (for example Mews), availability and prices come from it, so options reflect live inventory. The guest sees the options side by side and picks one; the quote status becomes Options Presented while they decide. When exactly one arrangement fits, Brixa selects it and prepares the quote straight away.

What a quote contains

  • Stay dates and the guest count
  • One or more room options with nightly and total prices
  • Included products and services, such as breakfast, city tax, or a welcome drink — your required items appear on every option
  • The cancellation policy that applies to the chosen rate
  • Any additional notes you add for the guest

Reviewing before it goes out

Brixa prepares the message that presents the quote as a draft in the conversation. Before sending, check the dates, guest count, prices, and included items, and edit anything that needs a human touch. Quotes waiting on your review show as ready to send until you approve them.

How the guest receives it

The guest gets a message in their chat, on WhatsApp, Instagram, or whichever channel they wrote on, containing a link to the quote. Opening the link shows the full offer, and from there the guest can pick an option, accept, or decline. Once delivered, the quote status changes to Sent.

Sending updated quotes when details change

If the guest changes dates, guest count, or preferences, Brixa retires the old options and prices the request again, so the guest never acts on outdated numbers. You can also edit a quote yourself; Brixa refreshes prices and included items to match and prepares an updated message for the guest.
