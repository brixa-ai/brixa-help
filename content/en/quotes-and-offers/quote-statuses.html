Quote Statuses Explained

Every quote carries a status that tells you where it stands, from gathering details to a confirmed booking. Use this reference to read any quote at a glance and know what to do next.

Status reference

StatusWhat it means for youWhat you can do
Missing InformationKey details (dates, guest count, or required guest data) are missing, so the quote cannot be priced yet.Wait for the guest's reply, or fill in the details yourself.
Awaiting PricingAvailability and prices are being prepared.Usually nothing — this resolves on its own. If it lingers, check the conversation for an alert.
Options PresentedBookable room options were shared with the guest, who has not chosen yet.Answer questions, or edit the booking details if the guest changes the request.
No Options AvailableNothing bookable matches the current request.Suggest different dates or occupancy, then update the booking details.
Options ExhaustedThe guest asked for more choices but has already seen the best available options.The options remain valid — help the guest pick one or adjust the request.
Ready to SendAn option is chosen and the quote is prepared, waiting for your review.Review the draft message and send the quote.
SentThe guest received the quote link in the chat.Monitor for a response, or send a reminder from the follow-up list if the guest goes quiet.
Accepted by GuestThe guest accepted the quote from the link. For hotels with automatic reservation creation, the hold is created and the quote moves to Tentatively Booked without any action from you.Check for missing guest data. If your hotel doesn't create holds automatically, create the hold in your PMS and mark the quote Tentatively Booked — or confirm the booking directly if you're ready.
Tentatively BookedThe room is held in your PMS while the guest completes payment or a guarantee, following the instructions shown on the quote.Nothing required while the guest pays. Once payment or a guarantee is confirmed, confirm the booking — in Brixa or in your PMS.
Confirmed by HotelYour team confirmed the accepted quote — the booking is settled.Nothing further on the quote; handle the reservation.
Rejected by GuestThe guest declined the quote.Ask what didn't fit; send a revised quote if there's still interest.
ExpiredThe validity period passed without a decision, or a tentative hold wasn't confirmed in time.Edit it to reopen, or send a fresh quote.
LapsedThe quote was closed on the hotel's side without being completed.Start a new quote if the guest is still interested.
CancelledYour team cancelled the quote.Nothing — send a new quote to restart.

Staying on top of status changes

You receive an email when a quote needs your attention — for example when a guest accepts or rejects it, or when it expires. See what happens after a guest accepts for the steps to take on an acceptance, and Quote Expiry and Follow-Ups for reminding guests who go quiet.
