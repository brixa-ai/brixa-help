Quote Statuses Explained

Every quote in Brixa carries a status that tells you exactly where it stands, from gathering details to a confirmed booking. Use this reference to read any quote at a glance and know what to do next.

Status reference

StatusWhat it meansWhat you can do
Missing InformationKey details (dates, guest count, or required guest data) are missing, so the quote cannot be priced yet.Wait for the guest's reply, or fill in the details yourself.
Awaiting PricingBrixa is fetching availability and prices, from your connected PMS when you have one.Usually nothing — this resolves on its own. If it lingers, check the conversation for an alert.
Options PresentedTwo or three bookable room options were shared with the guest, who has not chosen yet.Answer questions, or edit details if the guest changes the request.
No Options AvailableNothing bookable matches the current request.Suggest different dates or occupancy; a changed request triggers a fresh search.
Options ExhaustedThe guest asked for more choices but has already seen the best available options.The options remain valid — help the guest pick one or adjust the request.
Ready to SendAn option is chosen and the quote is prepared, waiting for review.Review the draft message and send the quote.
SentThe guest received the quote link in the chat.Monitor for a response; Brixa follows up if the guest goes quiet.
Accepted by GuestThe guest accepted the quote from the link.Check for missing guest data and confirm the booking.
Confirmed by HotelYour team confirmed the accepted quote — the booking is settled.Nothing further on the quote; handle the reservation.
Rejected by GuestThe guest declined the quote.Ask what didn't fit; send a revised quote if there's still interest.
ExpiredThe validity period passed without a decision.Edit it to reopen, or send a fresh quote.
LapsedThe hotel closed it, or a step on the hotel's side wasn't completed in time.Start a new quote if the guest is still interested.
CancelledYour team cancelled the quote.Nothing — send a new quote to restart.

How a quote moves between statuses

The usual path is: Missing Information → Awaiting Pricing → Options Presented → Ready to Send → Sent → Accepted by Guest → Confirmed by Hotel. Not every quote visits every stage — a single perfect match skips straight from pricing to Ready to Send, and whenever the guest changes dates or guest count, the quote returns to Awaiting Pricing so old prices are never reused. Rejected, Expired, Lapsed, and Cancelled close the quote, though guests can ask for more options even after a quote is sent, and expired quotes can be reopened by editing them. See what happens after a guest accepts for the acceptance flow.

Which changes notify staff

Your team receives an email when a status change comes from the guest or from Brixa itself — for example when a guest accepts or rejects a quote, or when a quote is sent or expires. Brixa notifies once per meaningful change, never twice for the same status. Changes you make yourself in the app don't trigger an email, since you already know about them. Quotes that go quiet also enter the follow-up list — see Quote Expiry and Follow-Ups.
