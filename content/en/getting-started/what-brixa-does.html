What Brixa Does

Brixa is an AI assistant for hotels that reads guest messages on channels like WhatsApp and Instagram, replies or drafts replies, builds and sends booking quotes, and hands off to your team when human judgment is needed.

From first message to booking

When a guest writes to your hotel, Brixa picks up the conversation and works it end to end:

  • Reads and understands the message. Guests can write in their own language — Brixa detects it and always replies in the language the guest uses. Voice messages are transcribed so nothing is missed.
  • Answers questions. It responds to questions about your rooms, amenities, policies, and services using the information your team has set up for the hotel.
  • Collects booking details. If a guest wants to stay, Brixa asks for whatever is missing — dates, number of guests, room preferences — until it has enough to make an offer.
  • Prepares and sends quotes. It builds a quote with suitable room options and pricing, presents it to the guest, and tracks their response.
  • Follows up. If a guest goes quiet or a quote is about to expire, Brixa sends a well-timed reminder so opportunities do not slip away.

What Brixa does not do alone

Your team stays in control. Depending on your hotel's settings, Brixa either sends routine replies on its own or prepares them as drafts for staff to review and approve before anything reaches the guest. Some situations are always handed to a person:

  • A guest explicitly asks to speak with a human.
  • The conversation needs a decision Brixa should not make on its own, such as an unusual request or a sensitive complaint.
  • Something blocks progress — for example, missing information Brixa cannot resolve by asking the guest.

When any of these happen, the conversation is flagged for your attention so a team member can step in. You can read more in When Brixa Escalates.

How Brixa fits your day

Brixa handles the repetitive volume — first responses, common questions, quote building, reminders — around the clock, including outside reception hours. Your team focuses on the moments that need people: approving drafts, resolving flagged conversations, and taking care of guests who need a personal touch. A short daily routine is usually enough; see Your Daily Workflow with Brixa.
