Your Daily Workflow with Brixa

Brixa handles most guest messages on its own, so a short, focused routine is enough to keep every conversation and quote moving. This article walks through a simple end-to-end workflow you can repeat each shift.

1. Review conversations that need attention

Start in the inbox. Rather than reading everything, focus on the conversations Brixa has surfaced for you — new activity, guests waiting on an answer, and anything flagged for review. Open each one, skim the recent exchange, and decide whether Brixa's handling is on track or you should step in. See Viewing Conversations for how to find and read threads.

2. Approve or edit waiting drafts

Depending on your hotel's settings, some of Brixa's replies wait as drafts for a human decision instead of going out automatically. For each draft:

  1. Read the guest's message and Brixa's suggested reply.
  2. Edit the reply if you want a different tone or detail — Brixa keeps the guest's language when it translates your changes.
  3. Send it, or discard it and write your own.

Clearing drafts early in the day keeps response times short while guests are actively planning.

3. Check quotes and guest responses

Next, look at the quotes area. Confirm that new offers went out, and give special attention to quotes where the guest has responded — an acceptance, a change request, or a question about the options presented. Quotes approaching their expiry date may deserve a personal nudge even though Brixa follows up automatically. See Creating and Sending Quotes.

4. Handle escalations

Conversations Brixa could not or should not finish alone are flagged for human handling — a guest asked for a person, a request fell outside what Brixa can decide, or something blocked progress. Work through these promptly: while a conversation is flagged, the guest is effectively waiting for your team. Handling Intervention Cases explains how to resolve each one and hand the conversation back to Brixa when appropriate.

5. Close out

Before you finish, do a quick sweep:

  • No drafts left waiting for approval.
  • No flagged conversations without an owner.
  • Accepted quotes are moving toward a reservation, with no missing guest details holding them up.
  • Anything you could not resolve is noted for the next shift.

A rhythm that scales

Many teams run this loop two or three times a day — morning, midday, and end of shift. Because Brixa keeps answering routine messages between your check-ins, the workload stays steady even on busy days: you supervise and decide, Brixa does the typing.
