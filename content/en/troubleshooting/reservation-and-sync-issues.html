Troubleshooting Reservation and Sync Issues

Quick fixes for bookings that haven't reached your PMS, reservations stuck on missing details, availability mismatches, expired holds, and temporary PMS outages.

An accepted booking hasn't appeared in the PMS

Likely cause: The booking isn't ready yet (required guest details are still missing), reservation writing isn't enabled for your hotel, or the PMS couldn't be reached at the moment of creation.

  1. Open the conversation in Brixa and check the booking panel — if details are missing, the reservation is intentionally on hold. See Reservation Readiness.
  2. Confirm reservation writing is switched on for your hotel; without it, staff enter bookings manually. See Connecting Your PMS.
  3. If the booking was ready and writing is enabled, treat it as a connection issue: enter the reservation in your PMS by hand using the details in Brixa, then see PMS Issues and Recovery.

A booking is stuck as "not ready"

Likely cause: A required guest detail — typically the full name or email address — is still missing. Brixa keeps asking the guest and will not move a half-filled booking into your systems.

  1. Check the guest details section of the booking to see exactly what is incomplete.
  2. If you already have the information, enter it yourself — that completes readiness immediately.
  3. If the guest has gone quiet, send a short personal follow-up. See Reservation Readiness and Quote Acceptances and Missing Guest Details.

Availability in quotes doesn't match the PMS

Likely cause: Brixa briefly holds on to recently fetched availability to keep quotes fast, so a change you just made in your PMS — closing a room, taking a walk-in — can take a few minutes to show up in new quotes. This short lag is normal, not an error.

  1. Wait a few minutes and check a new quote for the same dates.
  2. If the mismatch persists well beyond that, or quotes offer rooms your PMS says are sold out, contact Brixa's team. See What Syncs Between Brixa and Your PMS.

A hold or optional reservation expired

Likely cause: A pending hold has a time limit, and nobody finalized it before the limit ran out. The PMS released the room back to sale — normal behavior, but the guest no longer has a booking.

  1. Contact the guest to confirm they still want the stay.
  2. Create a fresh booking — availability and price may have changed since the original quote.
  3. Make reviewing open holds part of your daily routine so the next one doesn't lapse unnoticed. See Reservation States Explained.

The PMS connection is temporarily down

Likely cause: Your PMS is unreachable. Brixa never guesses availability or prices: it retries quietly, and if a guest is left waiting it flags the conversation for your team instead.

  1. Check your PMS directly — it is always the source of truth.
  2. Answer any flagged guests yourself using what the PMS shows.
  3. Enter urgent reservations manually; Brixa will not create duplicates of bookings you've already handled once the connection recovers.
  4. If the outage persists, contact support with the conversation reference. Full steps in PMS Issues and Recovery.
