Troubleshooting Quote Issues

Quick fixes for the most common quote problems — quotes stuck waiting on details, missing room options, expired offers, changed prices, and quotes that never seem to reach the guest.

The quote is stuck asking the guest for information

Likely cause: Brixa cannot price a stay until it knows the dates and the number of guests. The quote sits in Missing Information until those answers arrive, and Brixa keeps asking the guest for them.

  1. Open the conversation and check which details are still missing on the quote.
  2. If you already have the information — from a phone call, for example — enter it yourself so the guest isn't asked again.
  3. If the guest has gone quiet, a short personal message often unblocks them. See Quote Statuses Explained.

No room options are available for the dates

Likely cause: Nothing bookable matches the current request — the dates are sold out in your PMS, or the party size doesn't fit any room. The quote shows No Options Available.

  1. Check availability for those dates directly in your PMS.
  2. Suggest nearby dates or a different occupancy to the guest — a changed request triggers a fresh search automatically.
  3. If your PMS clearly shows open rooms, see Reservation and Sync Issues — the connection may be lagging or down.

The quote expired before the guest acted

Likely cause: Every quote has a validity period, and this one ran out without a decision. Brixa sends up to three reminders first, so the guest most likely stayed silent.

  1. If the guest returns and nothing has changed, edit the quote to reopen it — Brixa rechecks the details and puts it back in play.
  2. If dates, occupancy, or prices have moved on, send a fresh quote instead. See Quote Expiry and Follow-Ups.

The guest says the prices changed

Likely cause: The guest changed the request — different dates, guest count, or preferences — so Brixa retired the old options and repriced everything from live availability. Old numbers are never reused, on purpose.

  1. Compare the current request with the original one in the conversation.
  2. Explain to the guest that the new details were priced fresh; the earlier prices belonged to the earlier request. See Creating and Sending Quotes.

The quote won't send or never reached the guest

Likely cause: The messaging channel refused the message — most often because the guest's messaging window closed, or delivery failed on the channel's side.

  1. Check when the guest last wrote. Outside the window, WhatsApp needs a template and Instagram allows nothing — see Messaging Windows and Sending Limits.
  2. Look for a delivery failure flag on the conversation and follow Delivery Failures and Template Issues.

The guest accepted but nothing seems to happen

Likely cause: Acceptance is not the last step. Brixa may still be collecting required guest details, and your team must confirm the booking — Brixa never confirms on your behalf.

  1. Check the acceptance email or the quote in Brixa and confirm (or release) the room promptly.
  2. See what details are still missing in the booking panel — Brixa is likely asking the guest for them. See Quote Acceptances and Missing Guest Details.
  3. If the booking should already be in your PMS, see Reservation and Sync Issues.
