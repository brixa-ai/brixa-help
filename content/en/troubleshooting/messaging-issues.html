Troubleshooting Messaging Issues

Quick fixes for the most common messaging problems — replies that won't send, repeated delivery failures, missing drafts, conversations waiting on a human, and replies in the wrong language.

My reply won't send

Likely cause: The channel's messaging window has closed. After a guest's last message, WhatsApp allows free replies for 24 hours; once that passes, only pre-approved template messages go through, and Instagram allows effectively nothing until the guest writes again.

  1. Check when the guest last messaged you. If it was more than a day ago, the window is almost certainly closed.
  2. On WhatsApp, let Brixa send a template message to restart the conversation — a guest reply reopens the window.
  3. On Instagram, wait for the guest, or use another contact method you have on file. See Messaging Windows and Sending Limits.

A message keeps failing to deliver

Likely cause: The guest is unreachable (changed number, deleted account, blocked the hotel), a template is no longer approved, or the channel is having an outage. Brixa retries temporary failures; when retries run out, it flags the conversation for you.

  1. Open the flagged conversation and check what the guest was supposed to receive.
  2. Try a fresh reply — if the failure was temporary, it may go through now.
  3. Fall back to an email address or phone number from the quote or booking details.
  4. If several conversations fail at once, contact support — that pattern points to an account or channel problem. Full checklist in Delivery Failures and Template Issues.

The guest wrote, but no draft or answer appeared

Likely cause: Brixa deliberately held back. It may have escalated the conversation for human judgment, still be preparing a response, or the key information may be inside an attachment, which Brixa understands less well than text and voice notes.

  1. Check the conversation's status — if it is flagged as needing attention, Brixa is waiting for you. See Conversation Statuses.
  2. If the guest sent a photo or document, review it yourself before replying — see How Channels Work in Brixa.
  3. You can always reply manually without waiting for a draft. See Replying to Guests.

The conversation seems stuck waiting for a human

Likely cause: The conversation is escalated. While escalated, Brixa sends nothing automatically and pauses follow-ups until someone on your team steps in and hands it back.

  1. Open the conversation and read the banner explaining why Brixa asked for help.
  2. Resolve the case — reply to the guest, decide on the change, or fix the underlying issue.
  3. Resume automation so Brixa picks the conversation back up. See Handling Cases That Need You and When Brixa Escalates.

Replies are going out in the wrong language

Likely cause: Brixa replies in the language it detected from the guest's messages. Very short messages, mixed languages, or a guest switching mid-conversation can make the detected language differ from what the guest prefers.

  1. Check the guest's original messages (not the translation shown to you) to see what language they actually wrote in.
  2. Edit the draft before sending, or write your own reply in the right language — manual text goes out as you wrote it.
  3. If the mismatch persists, tell support the conversation reference. See Replying to Guests for how language handling works.
