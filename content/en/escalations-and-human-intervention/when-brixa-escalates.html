When Brixa Asks for Human Help

Brixa handles most guest messages on its own, but some situations call for a person. When that happens, Brixa flags the conversation, notifies your team, and waits for a human before doing anything else automatic on that conversation.

Situations that trigger an escalation

  • The guest asks for a person. If a guest explicitly asks to speak with a human or a team member, Brixa steps aside right away. It still prepares a suggested handoff reply for you, but it does not continue the conversation on its own.
  • The guest seems upset. Signs of frustration or anger hand the conversation to your team so a person can respond with care.
  • Sensitive or risky topics. Complaints, refund requests, and messages with concerning content are routed to staff rather than answered automatically.
  • Changes to confirmed bookings. If a guest wants to modify or cancel a reservation that is already confirmed, Brixa escalates so you decide how to handle it. See Modifications and Changes.
  • Brixa isn't confident. When the guest's request is unclear, or Brixa's own quality checks say a drafted answer isn't good enough, it asks for your review instead of guessing.
  • Requests Brixa can't fulfill yet. Some unusual booking setups — for example, several rooms each with different arrival and departure dates — are outside what Brixa can quote automatically.
  • Valuable opportunities. Signals of a group, corporate, or event booking are flagged so a person can give them proper attention.
  • System problems. If Brixa can't get reliable availability or pricing from your connected PMS, it stops rather than send a guest information that might be wrong. See Integration Errors and Recovery.

Urgency levels

Not every escalation is equally urgent, so notifications are paced accordingly:

UrgencyExamplesHow your team is notified
UrgentGuest asks for a human, upset guest, change to a confirmed booking, PMS problemImmediate email, with reminders within about 15 minutes if no one responds
StandardUnclear request, low confidence, unusual booking setup, group opportunityImmediate email, with reminders within about an hour if no one responds

Opening the flagged conversation counts as taking notice — reminder emails stop once someone on your team has seen it.

What happens while a conversation is escalated

An escalated conversation stays in human hands until someone on your team hands it back. During that time, Brixa does not send anything to the guest automatically and scheduled follow-ups are paused. It may still prepare drafts for you, and you can always reply yourself. When you're done, you resume automation and Brixa picks the conversation back up — see Handling Cases That Need You.
