When Guests Ask for Changes

Guests often want to adjust something after seeing a quote — different dates, more people, another room type, or an extra. Brixa understands these requests and prepares an updated quote, involving your team only when a change needs human judgment.

What guests can ask to change

A guest can request a change at any point — while options are still being discussed, after the quote was sent, or even after they accepted it. Typical requests include:

  • Different check-in or check-out dates, or a longer or shorter stay.
  • A different number of guests, such as adding a child.
  • A different room type or a different one of the presented options.
  • Extras and services, like breakfast, a late checkout, or an airport transfer.

How Brixa handles the request

Brixa reads the request in the guest's own words and works out what actually changed. What happens next depends on the kind of change:

Type of change What Brixa does
New dates, guest count, or room preferences Rebuilds the quote: checks availability and pricing again and presents fresh options that match the new request.
Picking a different option from those already presented Updates the quote to the chosen option — no rebuild needed.
Asking for more or different options Searches again; if nothing better exists, Brixa tells the guest these are the best available choices.
Adding or removing extras Adjusts the quote's contents and total, then presents the updated offer.

What happens to the previous quote

The conversation keeps one live quote, so the guest never juggles competing offers. When a change triggers a rebuild, the previously presented options are withdrawn and replaced by the new ones; the quote's status moves back to a preparation stage while pricing is refreshed. Earlier options remain visible to your team in the quote's history, so you can always see what the guest was offered before. See Quote Statuses for how these stages appear in Brixa.

Changes after acceptance

If the guest already accepted and then asks for a change, treat the previous acceptance as no longer standing: the updated quote must be presented and accepted again. If a reservation was already created in your PMS, check whether it needs updating there too — see Handling Cancellations for the related teardown steps.

When a change needs staff review

Brixa hands the conversation to your team instead of changing things automatically when:

  • No suitable options exist for the new request, or availability cannot be verified.
  • The request involves something outside standard offers — special pricing, exceptions to policy, or unusual arrangements.
  • The booking is already confirmed by the hotel, so changing it is a business decision.

Flagged conversations appear for your attention as described in When Brixa Escalates.
