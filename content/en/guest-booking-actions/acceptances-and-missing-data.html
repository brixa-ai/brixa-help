Quote Acceptances and Missing Guest Details

When a guest accepts a quote, Brixa alerts your team right away and gathers any guest details that are still missing, so the booking can move forward without delay.

What happens when a guest accepts

A guest can accept a quote directly from the quote page Brixa sent them, or by saying so in the conversation. Either way, the quote is marked as accepted and the clock starts: accepted quotes are time-sensitive, and a quick confirmation from your hotel keeps the guest confident and reduces the risk of losing the booking.

How your team is notified

Every active user at your hotel receives an email the moment a quote is accepted. The email summarizes the booking — guest name, dates, room type, number of guests, total amount, and days until check-in — and offers direct actions:

  • Confirm the booking if you can honor it.
  • Cancel as sold out if the room is no longer available.
  • View in Brixa to open the quote and the conversation.

If nobody acts within about 24 hours, Brixa sends a reminder email showing how long the acceptance has been waiting. Once someone confirms or cancels the quote, no reminder is sent.

When guest details are still missing

Sometimes a guest accepts before sharing everything needed to register the booking — most commonly their full name or email address. When that happens, Brixa messages the guest to request the missing details, in the guest's own language, before the booking moves forward.

Which details are required, and how far a booking can progress without them, is explained in Reservation Readiness.

What staff see meanwhile

While Brixa waits for the guest's answer, the acceptance is already visible to your team: the quote shows as accepted in Brixa, and the conversation shows the request Brixa sent. You do not need to chase the guest yourself in the normal case — Brixa handles the follow-up.

When to step in

  1. Confirm or release the room promptly. The acceptance email (and reminder) exists so a person makes this call — Brixa does not confirm bookings on your behalf.
  2. If the guest goes quiet, consider reaching out personally. A short human message often unblocks a guest who ignored the automated request.
  3. If the guest provides details that look wrong — a misspelled email, an incomplete name — correct them in the conversation or the quote before confirming.

If the guest asks for changes instead of providing the details, see When Guests Ask for Changes. For an overview of every status a quote can be in, see Quote Statuses.
