Handling Cancellations

When a guest decides not to go ahead, Brixa recognizes the cancellation, closes out the quote cleanly, notifies your team, and answers the guest — while your staff confirms policies and tidies up any reservation that already exists.

How Brixa recognizes a cancellation

Guests rarely use a single phrase. Brixa understands cancellation requests expressed naturally in the conversation — "we can't come anymore", "please cancel our booking", "we found something else" — as well as a formal decline made on the quote page itself. It also distinguishes a true cancellation from a request to change the booking; if the guest actually wants different dates or rooms, Brixa treats it as a change instead (see When Guests Ask for Changes).

What happens to the quote

The outcome depends on how far the booking had progressed:

Situation Outcome
Quote sent, not yet accepted The quote is marked as rejected by the guest and no longer counts as an open offer.
Quote accepted, not yet confirmed by the hotel The acceptance is withdrawn and the quote is closed; no further reminders are sent to your team about it.
Booking already confirmed by the hotel The quote is closed as cancelled, and your team should also handle the reservation itself (see below).

The conversation reflects the closed state, and the quote's final status stays visible for your records. See Quote Statuses for the full list.

How your team is notified

Cancellations and rejections trigger an email to your hotel's active users so nobody keeps holding a room for a booking that is gone. The notification links straight to the quote in Brixa, and where the guest gave a reason, it is included — useful for spotting patterns like price objections.

What staff should confirm

  1. Check the cancellation policy. If the quote carried a policy with fees or deadlines, decide whether they apply and communicate that clearly to the guest before treating the matter as closed.
  2. Clean up the reservation in your PMS. If a reservation was already created in your connected PMS, cancelling the quote in Brixa does not automatically remove it — cancel or update the reservation there as well. How reservations are created is covered in Reservation Flow.
  3. Release the inventory. Once the reservation is cancelled, make sure the room is sellable again for those dates.

How the guest is answered

Brixa replies to the guest in their own language, acknowledging the cancellation politely and leaving the door open for a future stay. If any policy consequence needs to be communicated — a fee, a non-refundable deposit — that is a judgment call for your team, so review the conversation and follow up personally rather than leaving it to the automatic reply. If the cancellation raises anything unusual, Brixa flags the conversation for review as described in When Brixa Escalates.
