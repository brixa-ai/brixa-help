The Booking Confirmation Email Your Guests Receive

When a booking reaches Confirmed by Hotel, guests who booked by email, through the Brixa Concierge or through the Booking Engine automatically receive a confirmation letter by email, so your team does not need to write one.

What the guest receives

  • A letter in the guest's own language, with the booking details: stay dates, room, guests, price and how the payment stands
  • Your hotel's name and branding
  • A link to the guest's booking page, where they can see the booking again at any time

What your team receives

Your hotel's contacts receive a copy of every confirmation letter. If the guest replies to the email, the reply goes to your hotel's contacts, so it lands with your team rather than in an unmonitored inbox.

Which guests receive it

  • Receive it: guests who booked by email, through the Brixa Concierge or through the Booking Engine
  • Do not receive it: guests on WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Facebook and X — they already have the booking in their conversation with you

The guest also needs an email address on file. If the conversation has no email address for the guest, no letter is sent, so ask for one before you confirm.

Who controls this

Which channels send the confirmation letter is managed by Brixa and is not a setting you change in your hotel. If you want it changed for a channel, contact Brixa support.
