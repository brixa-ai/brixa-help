The Booking Confirmation Email Your Guests Receive

When a booking reaches Confirmed by Hotel, guests who booked on a channel that sends it, including email, the Brixa Concierge and the Booking Engine, automatically receive a confirmation letter by email, so your team does not need to write one.

What the guest receives

  • A letter in the guest's own language, with the booking details: stay dates, rooms, guests and price, and how the payment stands when the booking makes it clear
  • Your hotel's name and branding
  • A link to the guest's booking page, where they can see the booking again at any time

The letter is sent once per booking. It is not sent again when the booking changes later.

Once a booking is confirmed, your team can send the letter again, or send it to a guest on any channel, with Send confirmation email in the booking's actions menu.

What your team receives

Your hotel's contacts receive a copy of every confirmation letter. If the guest replies to the email, the reply goes to your hotel's contacts, so it lands with your team rather than in an unmonitored inbox.

Which guests receive it

  • Receive it: guests on the channels that send it, which include email, the Brixa Concierge and the Booking Engine
  • Do not receive it: guests on WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Facebook, Messenger and X — they already have the booking in their conversation with you

A booking that was made elsewhere, and that Brixa only looked up for the guest, gets no letter.

The letter goes to the guest's email address, so make sure the guest's email address is on file. Without one, no letter is sent and your team gets no copy.

Who controls this

Which channels send the confirmation letter is managed by Brixa and is not a setting you change in your hotel. If you want it changed for a channel, contact Brixa support.
