From Accepted Quote to Reservation

When a guest accepts a quote, Brixa turns that acceptance into a real reservation — automatically in your connected PMS when that is enabled for your hotel, or as a clear handoff for manual entry when it is not.

The path after acceptance

  1. The guest accepts. The quote moves to its accepted state and the booking enters the reservation flow.
  2. Brixa checks readiness. The booking only moves forward once all required guest details are complete. If anything is missing, Brixa asks the guest for it first — see Reservation Readiness.
  3. The reservation is created. If your hotel has a connected PMS with reservation writing enabled, Brixa creates the reservation there with the accepted dates, room, guest count, and price. If not, the booking is handed to your team for manual entry.

When your hotel has a connected PMS

With PMS writing enabled, an accepted and ready booking becomes a reservation in your PMS without staff involvement. The reservation carries the details the guest agreed to in the quote: stay dates, the selected room option, the number of guests, and the total price. Depending on your setup, the reservation may first appear as a hold rather than a final confirmed booking — see Reservation States Explained for what each state means.

What staff should verify in the PMS

Automation removes the typing, not the responsibility. After a reservation appears, a quick check in your PMS is good practice:

  • The dates, room type, and guest count match the accepted quote in Brixa
  • The price and any included extras look correct
  • The guest's name and contact details are complete
  • The reservation's state is what you expect — finalize any hold that needs manual confirmation under your hotel's process

Once the reservation exists in your PMS, treat the PMS as the source of truth for that booking. Changes to the stay should follow your normal PMS procedures.

Notes and special requests

Anything captured as notes or special requests during the conversation — a late arrival, an anniversary, a dietary need — travels with the booking and appears in the notes of the PMS reservation. Front desk and operations teams see it where they already work, without needing to open Brixa.

When there is no PMS connection

If your hotel does not have a connected PMS, or reservation writing is not enabled, Brixa still does everything up to the point of creation: it secures the acceptance, completes the guest details, and presents the finished booking to your team. Staff then enter the reservation into their own systems manually, copying the details from the booking view in Brixa. The booking information stays available in the conversation, so you can always refer back to what the guest agreed to.
