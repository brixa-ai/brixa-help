Reservation Readiness

Before an accepted quote can become a reservation, Brixa checks that all the guest details your hotel needs are complete. This check is called readiness, and it protects your team from half-filled bookings landing in your systems.

What "ready" means

A booking is ready when two things are true:

  • The guest has accepted the quote. Acceptance is the guest's clear yes to a specific option, dates, and price.
  • All required guest details are complete. Brixa will not move a booking forward while key information is still missing.

Until both conditions are met, the booking stays with Brixa and does not move to your PMS or appear as a reservation anywhere else.

Which details are typically required

The exact list depends on your hotel's setup, but the details Brixa most commonly needs before a booking is ready are:

  • The guest's full name, as it should appear on the reservation
  • A contact email address
  • A phone number, if it was not already captured from the conversation channel
  • Confirmed arrival and departure dates
  • The number of guests, including children where relevant

Some hotels also require extras such as nationality or an estimated arrival time. If your property has additional requirements, Brixa treats those as part of readiness too.

How Brixa collects missing details automatically

When a guest accepts a quote but some required details are missing, Brixa does not stop or escalate right away. It continues the conversation and asks the guest for the missing information in a natural, friendly way, in the guest's own language. As the guest answers, Brixa fills in the details and re-checks readiness. Once everything required is complete, the booking moves forward on its own — no staff action needed in the normal case.

How staff can see what's missing and help

  1. Open the conversation in Brixa at app.brixa.ai and review the booking panel for the accepted quote.
  2. Look for the guest details section — incomplete items are shown so you can see exactly what is still needed.
  3. If you already have the information (for example, from a phone call), enter it directly so the guest is not asked again.
  4. If the guest has gone quiet, you can send a personal follow-up message asking for the remaining details.

Filling in a missing detail yourself has the same effect as the guest providing it: as soon as the required set is complete, the booking is ready and continues to the next step described in From Accepted Quote to Reservation.
