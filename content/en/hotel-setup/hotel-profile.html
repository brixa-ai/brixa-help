Setting Up Your Hotel Profile

Your hotel profile is the foundation Brixa builds on: the details you enter here shape how Brixa describes your property, answers guest questions, and presents quotes. Accurate, complete information means accurate, confident replies to guests.

Why the profile matters

When a guest asks "Where are you located?", "What time is check-in?", or "Do you take card payments?", Brixa answers from the information your team has saved in the platform. It does not guess or pull details from the internet. If something is missing or out of date, Brixa either gives the guest stale information or hands the question to your team — so a well-maintained profile directly reduces your workload.

What to fill in

Profile detailHow Brixa uses it
Hotel name and descriptionUsed when introducing the property and answering general questions about what your hotel offers.
Address and locationAnswers "where are you?" questions and helps guests with directions and nearby-area queries.
Contact details (phone, email)Shared with guests when they need to reach your team directly.
CurrencyAll prices in quotes are shown in your hotel's currency.
Time zoneKeeps dates and times in conversations and quotes aligned with your local time.
Arrival and departure detailsAnswers common check-in and check-out questions without staff involvement.
Logo and imagesAppear on quotes and other guest-facing pages, so guests see your brand — not a generic template.
Tone and brand voiceGuides how Brixa phrases its replies so messages sound like your hotel.

Languages

Guests can write to your hotel in their own language. Brixa detects the guest's language automatically and replies in it, while showing your team the conversation in the language they work in — so you do not need to list every language you support. Just make sure your descriptions are clear and complete; Brixa handles the translation.

Keeping it current

  1. Review your profile whenever something changes at the property — renovated areas, new contact numbers, seasonal opening hours.
  2. After any change, read the updated text as a guest would: is it clear, current, and unambiguous?
  3. Send yourself a test question on a connected channel to confirm Brixa's answer reflects the change.

A good habit is a quarterly profile review, plus an immediate update whenever operational details change.
