Rooms, Products, and Amenities

Room types, products, and amenities are the building blocks of every quote and every answer Brixa gives about your property. This article explains what to configure for each and how it shows up in guest conversations.

Room types

Each room type you configure tells Brixa what it can offer and to whom. For every room type, set:

  • Name and description — Brixa uses these to present the room in quotes and to answer questions like "Is the Deluxe room quiet?" or "Does it have a balcony?". Write descriptions you would be happy to see quoted back to a guest.
  • Occupancy limits — maximum total guests, plus separate limits for adults, children, infants, and pets. Brixa uses these to decide which rooms fit a party. If a family of five asks for a room that only sleeps four, Brixa will not offer it — it will look for a valid alternative or propose more than one room.
  • Bed setup and size — bed options and room size help Brixa answer practical questions ("Can we get twin beds?").
  • Photos — images appear alongside the room in quotes, which makes offers more persuasive.

Room types can be marked active or inactive. Inactive room types are never offered, so use this for rooms under renovation or seasonal closures.

Products and services

Products are everything you sell or include beyond the room itself: breakfast, city tax, parking, airport transfer, a cleaning fee, spa access. For each product you define:

SettingWhat it controls
TypeWhether it is a tax, fee, service, or facility — so it is presented appropriately in the quote.
Charge modelFree, included in the rate, or paid; and whether the price applies per night, per person, or once per stay.
Required in every quoteRequired products (for example, city tax or a mandatory cleaning fee) are automatically added to every option in every quote. You never have to remember to add them, and guests cannot remove them.
Guest can removeFor optional extras, controls whether the guest may drop the item when reviewing the quote.
Who it applies toSome products apply to all guests, others only to adults or children — for example, breakfast priced differently by age group.

Because required products are attached to every quote option automatically, keeping their prices current is essential: a stale city-tax amount will appear on every quote you send.

Amenities

Amenities describe what your hotel and rooms offer without a price attached: Wi-Fi, pool, air conditioning, accessibility features, parking availability. They exist at two levels — hotel-wide amenities and per-room amenities. Brixa uses them to answer everyday guest questions ("Is there a gym?", "Does the room have a hair dryer?") instantly and accurately. If an amenity is not listed, Brixa cannot confirm it and may pass the question to your team.

Keep it aligned

Review rooms, products, and amenities whenever prices or offerings change. Occupancy rules also interact with your guest age settings — see Guest Categories and Policies — and everything here flows into quotes, described in Creating and Sending Quotes.
