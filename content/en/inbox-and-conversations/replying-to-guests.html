Replying to Guests

Brixa answers many guest messages on its own and prepares drafts for the rest — you review, edit, and send drafts, or write your own reply and take over the conversation at any time.

Automatic replies vs. drafts

When a guest message arrives, Brixa decides how to handle it based on the situation:

  • Automatic reply — for routine requests, such as common questions or straightforward booking steps, Brixa sends the answer to the guest directly. You'll see the sent reply in the conversation.
  • Draft for review — when the situation calls for a check by staff, Brixa prepares a suggested reply and leaves it as a draft. Nothing reaches the guest until you approve it.
  • Handed off to you — when human judgment is needed, Brixa flags the conversation for staff and still prepares a suggested reply you can use as a starting point. See When Brixa Escalates.

Reviewing and sending a draft

  1. Open the conversation and read the guest's message and Brixa's draft reply.
  2. Edit the draft if you want to change wording or details. You can also ask Brixa to generate a new version, optionally giving it extra instructions about what the reply should say.
  3. Send the reply. It goes to the guest on the channel they wrote on.

If Brixa is still preparing a response, you can stop it and take over, or let it finish and then edit the result.

Writing your own reply

You can always reply manually instead of using a draft. Type your message and send it — the guest receives it on their channel just like an automatic reply. Replying yourself is the right move when the guest's request is unusual, sensitive, or when you simply know something Brixa doesn't.

Guest language handling

Brixa detects the language each guest writes in and replies in that language, whether the reply is automatic or a draft. You see a translation in your own language so you can review with confidence. If you edit a draft or write your own reply, Brixa keeps the message in the guest's language for you.

After you reply

Once a reply is sent, the conversation is considered answered and closes until the guest writes again — a new guest message reopens it automatically. If your reply included a quote, you can follow what happens next in Quote Statuses.

When sending is restricted

Channels like WhatsApp and Instagram limit when a business can message a guest — typically within a time window after the guest's last message. If a conversation has gone quiet for too long, sending may be restricted or require an approved template. See Messaging Windows and Limits.
