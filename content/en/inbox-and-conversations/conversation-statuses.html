Conversation Statuses

A conversation's status tells you whether a guest is waiting for an answer, whether the exchange is resolved, and whether Brixa needs a person to step in.

The statuses at a glance

StatusWhat it meansWhat changes itWhat staff should do
Open The guest is waiting for an answer. There may be a draft from Brixa ready for review, or the guest's message may still be unanswered. A new guest message opens (or reopens) the conversation. Review any draft and send it, or write your own reply. See Replying to Guests.
Closed The guest's last message has been answered and the exchange is resolved for now. This includes cases where Brixa replied automatically. Sending a reply — by Brixa or by staff — closes the conversation. Nothing. Closed conversations stay in your list for reference.
Needs attention Brixa has paused and handed the conversation to staff because human judgment is required — for example, an unusual request or a guest asking for a person. Brixa flags it when it decides not to continue on its own; it clears when staff handle the case. Read the conversation and respond. Brixa will not continue until a person has stepped in.

How conversations open and close

You don't manage these statuses by hand — they follow the conversation naturally:

  1. A guest writes on a connected channel such as WhatsApp or Instagram. The conversation opens.
  2. Brixa replies automatically or leaves a draft. When a reply is sent, the conversation closes.
  3. If the guest writes again — even weeks later — the same conversation reopens with its full history intact.

If the guest sends several messages in quick succession, Brixa treats them as one request and answers them together, so the conversation stays open until the whole set is addressed.

Escalated conversations need a person first

When a conversation is flagged as needing attention, Brixa stops replying on its own until staff have handled it. This protects guests from automated answers in situations that call for judgment — complaints, special requests, or anything Brixa isn't confident about. For the situations that trigger this and how to resolve them, see When Brixa Escalates.

Using statuses to plan your day

Work conversations flagged as needing attention first, since guests there are waiting for a person. Then go through open conversations with pending drafts. Closed conversations need no action — Brixa reopens them for you the moment a guest returns. For finding these in your list, see Viewing Conversations.
